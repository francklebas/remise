# Bug report guide

Use this template when reporting a problem in Boardly. A precise report makes the issue reproducible and easier to fix.

## Title

Write a short title describing the action and the result, for example: `Dragging a card to another column loses its due date`.

## Description

Explain what happened and what you expected to happen.

## Steps to reproduce

List the smallest reliable sequence of actions:

1. Sign in with the relevant account.
2. Open the affected workspace, board, or card.
3. Perform the action that triggers the problem.

## Context

Include:

- Environment: local or production, and the URL.
- Browser and version, operating system, and device.
- Workspace/board name, without sharing secrets.
- Whether the problem happens every time or intermittently.
- The exact error message, if one is visible.

## Evidence

Attach a screenshot or a short recording when it helps. Include browser console or network errors as text when available, after removing tokens, email addresses, and other private data.

## Expected result

Describe the correct behavior in one or two sentences.

## Actual result

Describe what Boardly did instead, including any data that was lost or changed.
